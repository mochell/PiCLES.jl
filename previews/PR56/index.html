PiCLES.jl

Particle-In-CelL for Efficient Swell — a fast surface wave model for Earth System Models, written in Julia.

PiCLES advances wave growth and propagation along Lagrangian particle trajectories, then uses Particle-in-Cell (PIC) remeshing to deposit a compact state vector (energy ε, energy-weighted group-velocity components m_x, m_y) back onto a regular ocean grid. The reduced state vector (~5 variables per particle vs. ~600 for a third-generation spectral model) is the key to its speed: depending on resolution, PiCLES is 1–4 orders of magnitude cheaper than WW3-class models while still resolving the dominant wind sea relevant to air–sea coupling.
